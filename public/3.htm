Dear Applicant, Your application has been applied successfully. Please follow the below mentioned steps.

01. Pay the required attestation Fee through 1Bill using any of Mobile Banking App/ Easypaisa /Jazz Cash)/United Bank Limited through its Challan/PSID.
02. After paying the fees, please visit the IBCC Islamabad office during the Express shift in the evening. Bring a printout of your application form along with the following documents:

  • Original Documents
  • CNIC/Passport copy
  • Original Fee Receipt
  • Verification must be forwarded by the board to IBCC's online portal.

Online Payment Instructions
Pay Your IBCC Fee Using 1 Bill


  1. Sign into your Internet/Mobile Banking
  2. Select Bill Payment & 1Bill
  3. Select Voucher/Invoice
  4. Add your given Challan/PSID
  5. Your bill details will automatically be fetched.
  6. Verify your details and press confirm/Pay Bill.
  7. You will be shown a confirmation message by your back on the screen.


Pay via ATM
  1. Insert your card and input your PIN on any ATM in Pakistan.
  2. Select Bill Payment & 1Bill.
  3. Select Voucher/Invoice.
  4. Add your given Challan/PSID
  5. Your bill details will automatically be fetched.
  6. Verify your details and press confirm/Pay Bill.
  7. You will be shown a confirmation message by your back on the screen.


Pay Through Easypaisa/Jazzcash
  1. Sign into your Account
  2. Select Bill Payment & 1Bill.
  3. Select Voucher/Invoice
  4. Enter your Challan/PSID
  5. Your bill details will automatically be fetched.
  6. Verify your details and press confirm/Pay Bill.
  7. You will be shown a confirmation message by your back on the screen
